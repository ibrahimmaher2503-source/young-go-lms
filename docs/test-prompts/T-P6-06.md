## Semantics
- Issuance happens on `CourseCompleted` only (auto and manual approval). A completion with any certificate row, even a revoked one, is never auto-issued again; a staff revoke stays until staff re-issue.
- Download: the learner, or a parent with a verified, active link to that learner (`GuardianAccess`), gets the PDF; any other signed-in user, a missing id and a revoked certificate get 404. Guests are redirected to login.
- Verify page shows name, course (current locale, falling back to the other), completion date, number and status only. No email, phone, ids, `revoked_reason` or token echo. Revoked shows "revoked". Unknown token 404.
- Reissue notifies nobody (the issuance notice is once per completion).

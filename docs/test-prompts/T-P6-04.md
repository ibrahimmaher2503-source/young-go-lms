## Behavior
- Who may review: active user with `assessment.manage` who passes the `update` gate on the course (instructor with `manage` scope on the course, or an authorized admin). Lists and the review pages only resolve records of such courses: other instructors see nothing and get 404 on the review URL; every Action re-checks and throws `AuthorizationException`.
- Decisions on the current revision, allowed while status is submitted, resubmitted, graded, accepted or rejected (re-deciding is allowed and adds a new row). Not allowed on draft or returned (`not_decidable`).
  - grade: score 0-100 required -> `graded`. accept: score optional -> `accepted`. reject: feedback required -> `rejected`. request revision: feedback required, no score -> `returned`; refused when `revisionsLeft` is 0 (`no_revisions_left`).
  - Score range 0-100 (`score_range`). Scores are a working default (assignments have no points field).
  - Late penalty: taken from the reviewed revision (`is_late` and `late_penalty_percent`), `final_score = score * (100 - penalty) / 100`, stored with the raw score and penalty. No penalty for on-time or allowed-late work.
  - Feedback text becomes a `submission_feedback` row tied to the decision and revision. `AddSubmissionFeedback` adds a comment without a decision (not on drafts), audited, no notice.
- Audit per decision: event `assignment.<decision>` on the assignment, before `{status, score}`, after `{status, score, raw_score, late_penalty_percent}`, context submission, decision and revision. If the audit cannot be written the decision rolls back.
- Notification: one `SubmissionDecisionNotice` per decision, dedupe key `assignment-decision:{decision id}`, through `NotificationDispatcher` (so a child without email is notified in app and verified guardians get the email copy). A second decision sends a second notice; a failing send never undoes the decision.
- Essay grading (`GradeEssayAnswer`): only essay questions with a written answer on a submitted attempt; points 0..question points; sets `points_awarded`, `is_correct` (full marks), `feedback`, `graded_by`, `graded_at`; recomputes the attempt through `QuizScoring` (needs_review clears when no written essay lacks points, `passed` is computed only then). `require_pass` quizzes complete the item through `CompleteAssessmentItem` (`quiz_passed`) once the attempt passes; `after_submission` was completed at submit and is not repeated. Audit `quiz.essay_graded` with before/after points, percent and passed. One `EssayReviewedNotice` when the review first completes (key `quiz-review:{attempt id}`); regrades are audited only.
- Queues: assignment queue filters course (only reviewable courses), status, overdue. Overdue = status submitted or resubmitted and the assignment `due_at` has passed. Essay queue filters course and state (default waiting for grading; reviewed). Summary strips count within the course filter.
- Learner view: statuses `accepted` and `rejected` are locked (not editable, no submit); card shows decision text, final score and late penalty, and the feedback thread; `returned` still shows the thread and allows resubmit.

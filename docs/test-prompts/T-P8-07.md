## Acceptance
- A1 Bundle purchase grants each course once: order with a bundle line for a learner, approve the manual payment (or call `GrantOrderEntitlements` with `Purchase`); one `bundle` entitlement per member course, `EntitlementService` allows every course; granting twice adds no row and no second audit; a learner already holding one course by another source gets entitlements for the rest, no duplicate for that course, and its allocation row has `skipped_existing = true` and null `entitlement_id`; a course line plus a bundle containing that course on one order gives one entitlement; a parent buying for two children gives each child their own.
- A2 Allocation totals equal bundle net price: for several bundles (equal weights, uneven weights, price not divisible, price below the member count, price-based weights) the sum of `allocated_minor` over the item's allocations equals the bundle price, `order_items.allocated_minor` and `orders.total_minor`; no share is negative; ties go to the lower course id; `AllocateBundlePrice::split` rejects empty or zero weights; `OrderItemAllocations::for($item, $net)` for a partial amount (refund) sums to `$net` and keeps the proportions.

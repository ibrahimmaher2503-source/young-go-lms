## Money definitions (reconcile against these, nothing else)
- Sales: orders with `paid_at` not null and inside the range (status paid, partially_refunded, refunded, disputed all have it). Row amounts are the whole order (`subtotal`, `discount`, `total` in major units, minor / 100). `refunded` = sum of that order's refunds with status `executed` (any date). `net` = total - refunded. Totals (`gross`, `refunded`, `net`, `orders`) are per currency and equal the sums of the rows; EGP and SAR are never added together.
- Refunds report: refunds with status `executed` and `executed_at` inside the range; requested/approved/rejected/failed/processing are excluded.
- Instructor earnings: ledger entries with `earned_at` in the range, signed sums by type (`earnings`, `reversals`, `adjustments`), `net` = their sum; `accrued` (every status not pending/approved/held/paid) + `in_settlement` (pending + approved) + `held` + `paid` = `net` per row. Over a range that covers all entries of an instructor the totals equal `LedgerTotals::forInstructor($id)` per currency.
- Market: filters `orders.market` for sales, refunds and reconciliation (via the order), and the market's currency for earnings; it is ignored (not an error) for the other reports.

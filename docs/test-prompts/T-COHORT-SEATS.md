## What was built
- Table `cohort_seat_holds` (cohort_id, learner_id, order_item_id unique, status `held|converted|released`, expires_at, converted_at, released_at, release_reason `expired|cancelled|failed|full_at_payment`); model `Cohorts\CohortSeatHold` (`scopeActive` = held and not expired).
- Config `config/commerce.php`: `seat_hold_minutes` (default 30).
- `Cohort::heldCount(?User $except)` (distinct learners with an active hold who are not already seated); `Cohort::isFull(?User $for)` and `seatsLeft()` now count seated + live holds. `ReserveCohortSeat`, `JoinWaitlist`, `PromoteFromWaitlist` use it (a learner's own hold never blocks them). `CohortAvailability` (filters and `forCourses` badges) subtracts live holds from seats left. `CohortAvailability::enrollingFor($courseId)` lists enrolling cohorts, earliest start first.
- Cohorts actions: `HoldCohortSeat` (locks the cohort row, idempotent per order item, renews, audit `cohort.seat_held`, returns null if the learner already has a seat), `ReleaseSeatHolds` (reasons, `$onlyExpired`, audit `cohort.seat_released`), `ConvertSeatHolds` (audit `cohort.seat_converted`; if full at payment the learner goes to the front of the waitlist, the hold is released with `full_at_payment` and `cohort.seat_conversion_failed` is audited; never throws).
- `OrderLine` is unchanged (the existing test pins its constructor); the buyer does not choose a cohort. `CreateOrder`: for a **course** line whose course has enrolling cohorts (open status and window): the first enrolling cohort with room (earliest start, then id) is held; none with room = `ValidationException` on `order` with `commerce.cohort_full` and no order, items or holds left behind. A learner already seated in an enrolling cohort of the course needs no hold. A course with no enrolling cohort, and bundle/plan lines, hold nothing (unchanged).
- `TransitionOrder`: to `paid` converts the order's holds (seated `CohortMember`, hold `converted`); to `failed` or `cancelled` releases them (reason `failed` / `cancelled`). `ExpireAbandonedOrders` cancels through it, so it releases too.
- `StartCheckoutOrder` reuses an open order and calls `RenewOrderSeatHolds` (renews live holds and holds that expired by timeout; cohort full meanwhile = `commerce.cohort_full` error on `order`).
- `ReleaseExpiredSeatHolds` + command `orders:release-expired-seat-holds` (scheduled every minute): releases expired holds (reason `expired`); extends instead when the order has an open gateway attempt (`active_order_id` set) or a pending manual proof; releases when the order is no longer pending.
- Strings (AR and EN): `commerce.cohort_full`, audit labels `cohort.seat_held|seat_released|seat_converted|seat_conversion_failed`. The checkout page shows the error through the existing `order` error bag.

## Acceptance
- A1 Hold on create: a 3-seat cohort course; a learner creates an order: one `held` hold for the cohort and the order item, `expires_at` = now + 30 min (and follows `commerce.seat_hold_minutes` when changed), audit `cohort.seat_held`; `seatsLeft()` is 2; no `CohortMember` yet. Same idempotency key returns the same order and still one hold.
- A2 Refuse when full: capacity 1, learner A holds it, learner B gets the `commerce.cohort_full` message (AR and EN) on the `order` error key; no order, order item, audit `order.created` or hold exists for B. The same when the seat is taken by a seated member. Capacity null (no limit) always holds. A cohort full only because of an **expired** hold accepts B.
- A3 Last seat sequence: capacity 2 with 1 seated; A orders (gets it), B orders (refused); A's order is cancelled, then B's retry succeeds. Hold counts distinct learners: the same learner with two pending orders for the course uses one seat.
- A4 Cohort choice: two enrolling cohorts, first full: the second is held; all full: `cohort_full`. Closed cohorts or cohorts outside their window are never held. No enrolling cohort (closed or all outside window) or no cohort at all: order created as before, no hold.
- A5 Pay converts: paid through `TransitionOrder` (manual approval and the Fake webhook paths): hold `converted`, `CohortMember` `seated` (`seated_at` set), `cohort.seat_converted` audited, `seatedCount()` rises by one while `heldCount()` falls (no double count), `hasSeat()` true so cohort live sessions open. Transitioning to paid twice is a no-op. Coupon redemption and entitlement behaviour are unchanged.
- A6 Release: `failed` and `cancelled` transitions release the hold with the right `release_reason` and `cohort.seat_released`; the seat is available to another buyer at once. `ExpireAbandonedOrders` (24 h) cancels and releases. Bundle and plan orders create no hold.
- A7 Expiry command: after 31 minutes `orders:release-expired-seat-holds` releases the hold (reason `expired`), audit once, output counts; run again = nothing. A hold on an order with an open gateway attempt or a pending manual proof is extended by 30 minutes, not released; with the proof approved or rejected it is released at the next run. A converted hold is never touched. The schedule contains the command (everyMinute, withoutOverlapping).
- A8 Late payment: hold expired and released, seat still free, then the order is paid: seat is taken (hold `converted`). Seat taken meanwhile by B: payment still succeeds (order `paid`, entitlement granted), A is `waitlisted` at the front, hold `released` with `full_at_payment`, audit `cohort.seat_conversion_failed`. A hold released for `cancelled` or `failed` is not revived by a later paid transition of another path.
- A9 Renew on return: pending order whose hold expired; `StartCheckoutOrder` for the same product renews it (new `expires_at`, status `held`) when room remains and throws `commerce.cohort_full` (order unchanged) when B took the seat. A live hold is just extended.
- A10 Other flows respect holds: with a hold in place a manual `ReserveCohortSeat` for another learner on the last seat throws `CohortFullException`, the holder's own `ReserveCohortSeat` succeeds (and the hold stops counting); `JoinWaitlist` is allowed when full by holds; `PromoteFromWaitlist` does not take a held seat. `CohortAvailability::forCourses` and the `seats` / `open` filters drop a cohort whose last seat is held and show it again after expiry. Checkout page (`checkout.start` POST) shows `commerce.cohort_full` through the error bag in `ar` and `en`.

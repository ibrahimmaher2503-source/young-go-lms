## What was built
- Migration `2026_11_23_200000_create_course_revisions_table`: `course_revisions` (`course_id`, `author_id`, `number`, `status` draft|pending|approved|rejected|discarded, `base` JSON = live content when opened, `payload` JSON = proposed content, `open_course_id` unique = course id while draft/pending else null, `submitted_at`, `reviewed_at`, `reviewer_id`, `reason`, `applied_at`). Model `App\Models\CourseRevision`, enum `App\Domain\Catalog\Revisions\CourseRevisionStatus`, policy `CourseRevisionPolicy` (`update` = course update, `publish` = course `publish` and never the author).
- Content model (`RevisionSnapshot`): course fields `title, short_description, description, category_id, level, content_language, min_age, max_age, faqs, requirements, outcomes` and the active curriculum (sections with `id, title, published, items[{id, kind lesson|quiz|assignment|live_session, title, description, lesson_type, status}]`). Pricing, access, status, ownership, media, SEO, custom fields, lesson body/media/files and quiz questions are NOT part of a revision.
- Actions in `app/Domain/Catalog/Revisions/Actions`: `OpenCourseRevision($actor, $course, ?$continueFrom)` (published course only, one open revision, audit `course.revision_opened`; passing the latest rejected revision starts from its payload), `SaveCourseRevision($actor, $revision, array $input)` (draft only; `RevisionContent::normalize` validates; ids must belong to the base snapshot, otherwise `ValidationException` `course_revisions.stale_structure`), `SubmitCourseRevision` (draft to pending, needs at least one change, audit `course.revision_submitted`, event `CourseRevisionSubmitted`), `DiscardCourseRevision` (draft or pending, audit `course.revision_discarded`), `ApproveCourseRevision` / `RejectCourseRevision` (pending only, reviewer is not the author, reject needs a reason) and `ReviewCourseRevision($actor, $revision, 'approved'|'rejected', $reason)` which wraps them in `RecordReviewDecision` (ability `publish`, audit `course.revision.review.approved|rejected`). Approve also writes `course.revision_applied` and fires `CourseRevisionReviewed`.
- `ApplyCourseRevision` (called by approve inside its transaction): updates course fields; updates sections, lessons, quizzes, assignments **in place** (ids unchanged), creates new sections/items (`curriculum_items.sort` set, status from the revision), moves items between sections, re-sorts, archives (never deletes) removed sections/lessons/quizzes/assignments (live sessions are never removed or edited by a revision), and writes only what the revision changed against its `base` so edits made live in the meantime survive. Publishing an item runs `LessonPublishRule::assertPublishable` (failure rolls the approval back).
- `RevisionAccess::requiresRevision($actor, $course)` = course `published` AND owner `independent` AND actor lacks the `publish` ability on it. `assertLiveEditable` throws `ValidationException` key `course` (`course_revisions.use_revision`) and is called by `SaveCourseWizard` (when a course is given), `CurriculumAccess::authorizeStructure` (used by `ManageSections` add/rename/setPublished/archive, `ManageLessons` add/archive, `ReorderCurriculum`, `MoveCurriculumItem`, `SetCurriculumStatus`, `ManageAssessmentItems`) and `ManageLessons::update` when the lesson title/description actually change. Media/PDF/resource actions are unchanged (PDF versions still need document approval before becoming active).
- Direct edits (YG-GAP-CHG-002): a `SaveCourseWizard` save of a published course by a user who may publish it records audit `course.updated_live` (before/after of the changed attributes; none when nothing changed).
- Notifications (matrix rows `course.revision_submitted`, `course.revision_approved`, `course.revision_rejected`, group `course_review`, mandatory, placeholders `course`, `revision`, `reason` for rejected): `NotifyOfCourseRevision` sends submitted to every active user with `course.publish` + `panel.admin.access` who passes `publish` on the course (not the author); approved/rejected to the author, the course's instructors and the owner instructor (rejection body contains the reason). Notices use dedupe keys per revision and user.
- Filament: `ManageCourseRevision` (route `courses/{record}/revision` in admin and instructor panels; header actions `openRevision`, `editRevision` (slide-over, `CourseRevisionForm`), `submitRevision`, `discardRevision`; shows the open revision, compare table (`data-revision-compare`, rows `data-change-scope/type`), history of the last 10 (`data-history-row`)); `CourseRevisionResource` (admin panel, `/admin/course-revisions`, needs `course.publish` and admin panel access, default filter pending, row actions `compare`, `approve`, `reject` from `ApproveRejectAction`, hidden for non-pending and for the author); banner `data-revision-banner` on the curriculum manager; `EditCourse::mount` redirects an author who needs a revision to the revision page; `Courses` table has a `revision` row action for published courses.
- Lang: `lang/{ar,en}/course_revisions.php`, notification templates, audit event labels.

## Acceptance tests to write
- A1 Students see old content until approval: with a published course, learner entitlement and a lesson, an independent instructor opens a revision, saves new course title, lesson title and a new lesson, submits; `PublicCourseDetail`/`CurriculumOutline::forStudents`/`EntitledCurriculumQuery` and the course row still show the old values and none of the new items; after approval they show the new values.
- A2 Approve applies all changes in one transaction: approval with a change set (course field, section rename, new section, new lesson, removed lesson, reorder, item moved to another section, status change) lands completely; then force a failure (a published new lesson that `LessonPublishRule` blocks, e.g. paid course with a protected-tier media lesson, or a mocked listener) and assert nothing changed and the revision stays `pending`. Assert row ids of existing sections/items/lessons are unchanged and a lesson progress row / entitlement-backed access (via `EntitlementService`, not by touching the entitlements table) keeps working; removed items are archived (`archived_at`), not deleted.
- A3 Rejection reason notified to the instructor: reject with fewer than 10 chars fails (still pending, no audit); valid reject sets `rejected`, stores `reason`, audit `course.revision.review.rejected` with the reason in context, notification `course.revision_rejected` (body contains the reason) to author and course instructors; live course unchanged; the instructor can `OpenCourseRevision` again with the rejected revision as `continueFrom` and the payload equals the rejected one.
- Rules: one open revision per course (second open fails `already_open`, also at the DB level); only published courses can be opened; saving/submitting a non-draft revision fails; empty submit fails `no_changes`; ids not in the base snapshot are refused; author cannot approve/reject own revision; a user without the course `publish` grant (or an admin lacking `course.approve` on an independent course) gets `AuthorizationException`; discard from draft and from pending frees the course for a new revision; live-edit concurrency: change the live course title after opening, approve a revision that only changes the lesson title and assert the live title edit survives.
- Guards: an `instructor_independent` manager of a published independent course gets `ValidationException` (`course`) from `SaveCourseWizard`, `ManageSections::add/rename/archive`, `ManageLessons::add/archive`, `ManageLessons::update` with a changed title, `ReorderCurriculum`, `SetCurriculumStatus`, `ManageAssessmentItems`; the same actions still work for the same instructor on a draft course, for admins who pass `publish`, and `ManageLessons::update` without a title change still works. `RevisionAccess::requiresRevision` is false for academy-owned published courses.
- Direct edit audit: an admin with `course.publish` saves the wizard on a published academy course (changed title): one `course.updated_live` activity with before and after; saving with no change adds none.
- Notifications: submit notifies admins with publish rights (not the author, not admins lacking approve on an independent course), approve notifies the author and instructors; duplicates are not sent twice; a failing dispatcher never undoes the decision.
- Filament (Livewire): `ManageCourseRevision` as the instructor: `openRevision`, `editRevision` (state set from payload, change title and add an item, save), `submitRevision`; unassigned instructor gets 403/404; view-only instructor gets 403. `ListCourseRevisions` as admin: pending revision listed, `approve` and `reject` (reason required) work, row actions hidden for the author and for non-pending. `EditCourse` redirects the independent author to the revision page. Banner visible on the curriculum manager when a revision is open or required.

## Known limits to record, not to test as features
Lesson body, media, resources, subtitles, quiz questions and cohort/live-session scheduling are not revisioned (existing actions, existing document approval for PDFs); live-session items are kept and ordered but not edited or removed by a revision; the history shows the last 10 revisions only (spec: out of scope beyond that); a revision started before another structural live edit may refuse an id that no longer exists (`stale_structure`) and must be discarded.

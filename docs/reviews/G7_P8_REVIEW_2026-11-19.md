# G7 visual review, P8 payments, finance and workspace screens (2026-11-19)

Independent reviewer (did not build the UI). No application code, CSS, lang, tests or config was changed and nothing was committed. Screenshots: `tests/Browser/screenshots/G7-P8-review/<screen>-<ar|en>-<1440|390>.png` (full page, PNG, light theme only).

## Method and environment
- Scratch SQLite DB outside the repo, `migrate --force` + `demo:seed`, `php artisan serve` on 127.0.0.1:8099, `APP_ENV=local`, `CACHE_STORE=array` for the main run (see D1 for why), Playwright Chromium, Arabic = `dir=rtl`, English = `dir=ltr`.
- Accounts from `docs/DEMO.md`. Staff accounts are 2FA-enforced (`/security/two-factor`), so in the scratch DB only I set a known TOTP secret on admin, finance and instructor users to get past the challenge. The learner account hit "maximum devices" after repeated logins; I cleared `user_devices`/`user_sessions` in the scratch DB.
- Rubric: `agents/VERIFICATION_GATES.md` G7 (compare to reference at comparable viewport/language/theme) plus the `ux-reviewer` bar (every score at least 4, average at least 4.5). Dimensions scored 1-5: **Ref** reference or sibling match, **Vis** visual polish (tokens, type, spacing), **RTL** RTL/LTR parity, **Resp** responsive/overflow, **Sta** states and content, **Con** consistency with sibling screens.
- References used: 05 catalog, 06 course detail, 11 checkout, 10 finance ledger, 01 admin overview, 14 manual payment, plus 18 component library for the rest. No reference exists for: my/payments, my/subscription, fake gateway, refunds, settlements, statements, reports, notification templates, settings hub, finance/instructor dashboards. Those were judged against design-system rules and sibling screens (lower confidence, noted "sib").
- Method for reading: I inspected a side-by-side contact sheet per screen (AR 1440, EN 1440, AR 390, EN 390) and opened single full-size captures for the checkout reference, the 500 page and the finance 390 dashboard. Fine-grained pixel measurements (spacing in px, contrast ratios) were NOT taken; scores are visual judgements.
- Result: **no screen reaches 4.5. G7 stays OPEN for P8.**

- `php -S ... public/router.php` from the task does not work (no `public/router.php` in the repo); `php artisan serve` was used.

- Contact sheets used for review were kept in the scratchpad, not in the repo.

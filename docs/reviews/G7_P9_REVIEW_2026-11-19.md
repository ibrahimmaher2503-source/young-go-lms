# G7 visual review, P9 screens (2026-11-19)

Independent reviewer (did not build or fix the UI). No application code, CSS, lang, tests or config was changed and nothing was committed. Method and rubric as in `docs/reviews/G7_P8_REVIEW4_2026-11-19.md`: six dimensions (Ref, Vis, RTL, Resp, Sta, Con; 1-5), strict bar mean >= 4.5, PO per-class bar (4.5 with a pack reference, 4.3 without, every dimension >= 4.0). Scores are visual judgements, not pixel-measured. Screenshots: `tests/Browser/screenshots/G7-P9-review/<screen>-<ar|en>-<1440|390>.png` (106 files).

**Result: no screen group reaches 4.5 (strict). No group passes the per-class bar. Group scores run 3.97 to 4.15 (mean 4.10).** Five groups have every dimension >= 4.0. The weakest spots are a sidebar type-scale bug that touches every staff screen, raw field tokens in validation and Learner 360 text, empty-state dead ends (agreement, consent) and inconsistent learner chrome.

## Method and environment
- Scratch SQLite DB in the session scratchpad (never `database/database.sqlite`), `migrate --force`, `demo:seed` (the DemoSeeder command), `APP_ENV=local`, `php artisan serve --port=8096`. Staff 2FA was enabled in the scratch DB only (known TOTP secret) so the staff panels could be logged in; learner/instructor/admin/finance sessions are the demo accounts from `docs/DEMO.md`.
- Seeded extras (scratch only, through app Actions): Nour's wishlist (4 courses), one open "download my data" request submitted through the real form, a view-as session opened through the real modal.
- Playwright Chromium (repo `node_modules`), AR and EN, 1440x900 and 390x844, full-page captures, light theme only. All 92 main captures returned HTTP 200, `dir` rtl/ltr as expected, and `scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at both widths (no page-level horizontal overflow; the clipping noted below is inside components). No console errors.
- Capture artefacts (not defects): the fixed learner tab bar and the sticky mobile buy bar show mid-page in full-page captures.
- Another agent edited app files in parallel; no 500 was met.

## Blockers and gaps
- No hard blocker. 0 of 14 groups reach 4.5 under either bar; the nearest is notification preferences (4.15).
- No learner onboarding flow exists, so "onboarding" was mapped to instructor onboarding. The `/teach` success state, the agreement acceptance (no agreement published in the demo data) and the payout form itself were not captured.
- Cookie banner and consent toggles could not be captured (the app shows none while no marketing or analytics use exists).
- Not captured: dark theme, axe, contrast ratios, view-as course page (`/support/view-as/courses/{course}`), admin review/approve modals, wishlist with zero items, notification-preferences saved state, and the three unscored admin list pages above.
- The scratch DB, saved sessions and the 8096 server were created for this review only; the server was stopped at the end.

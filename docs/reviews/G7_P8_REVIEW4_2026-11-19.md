## Method and environment
- Scratch SQLite DB `rv4.sqlite` in the scratchpad (never `database/database.sqlite`), `migrate --force`, `demo:seed`, `APP_ENV=local`, repo-default `CACHE_STORE=file`, `public/build` already current (no source newer than the manifest), `storage:link` present. Server: `php artisan serve --port=8097`, `APP_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8097` (port 8099 was already held by a pre-existing listener, so I did not use it).
- Seeded through app Actions: 3 refunds (executed / approved / pending), 3 ledger adjustments (back-dated to 2026-10-15), instructor payout profile, 2 October statements, 1 draft settlement batch; the demo seed supplies 2 coupons, 1 bundle, 1 plan and 1 active subscription (Nour). New this round: a transfer proof submitted through the real form (order 9, EGP 1,200.00, pending) so the staff queue has a row. 2FA TOTP secrets set for staff in the scratch DB only.
- Playwright Chromium via node scripts (repo `node_modules`). 1440x900 and 390x844, AR and EN, light; dark for catalog, checkout order and admin dashboard. Full-page captures except `tabbar-*` (viewport). Read through contact sheets (AR 1440, EN 1440, AR 390, EN 390) for every screen.
- Capture caveats: the fixed learner tab bar and the sticky mobile price bar render mid-page in full-page captures (artefact, not a defect). Catalog images are `loading="lazy"`: the last two cards look blank in full-page captures but load on scroll (verified `naturalWidth` 1280 for all 8), so not a defect. Staff sessions expired between runs; I re-logged in and re-captured, and every staff PNG in the set shows its real page (all HTTP 200, no login pages).
- Not captured (gaps): refund and payment receipt pages, `/admin/login` and 2FA pages, axe runs, contrast ratios, a manual-payment approve/reject modal, populated reconciliation mismatches (the page was only seen in its "has not run yet" empty state).

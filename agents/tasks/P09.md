### T-P9-08 — Privacy rights, retention and staging anonymizer
- deps: T-P8-06, T-P7-04
- agents: implementer, test-writer
- size: M
- risk: high
- ai: hook — AI conversations included in export/deletion; AI retention pruning
- refs: YG-GAP-PRV-001..003, O-025
- gates: G1-G6

**Goal** Data export/deletion requests, retention pruning and safe staging data.
**Steps**
1. `data_requests` workflow: export (requester-verified, subject-scoped zip; AI conversation export only to an authorized subject under O-016, never automatically to guardians) and deletion (anonymize personal fields; keep financial/certificate records marked anonymized).
2. `retention_policies` + scheduled pruning per data class.
3. `app:anonymize` command for staging refresh (names, emails, phones, files placeholders).
**Acceptance**
- A1 Deletion leaves orders intact but anonymized (test).
- A2 Pruning removes only expired records per class (test).
- A3 Anonymizer leaves no real emails/phones (test).
**Out of scope** Legal review (O-025, YG-GAP-PRV-004).

**Review requirement — YG-REV-PRV-001 (PRD §29.15)**
Export/deletion is requester-verified and subject-scoped. Approved per-class retention and legal holds govern primary data, derived AI data, storage and backup expiry. Private staging imports remain access-restricted until sanitized; notifications and public certificate/review fields follow minor privacy policy, without full AI history.

**Additional acceptance** Cross-child export, revoked guardian, expired download link, held finance record, repeated deletion, pending jobs, sanitized staging and restore reapplication of deletion ledger. Production destructive pruning and minor public fields remain gated by O-015/O-016/O-025. Evidence is required; this task remains todo until implemented and verified.

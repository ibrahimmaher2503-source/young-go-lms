# P5 — Cohorts, live & hybrid learning

### T-P5-01 — Cohorts, capacity and waitlist
- deps: T-P4-01, T-P3-01
- agents: implementer, ui-builder, test-writer
- size: M
- risk: high
- ai: none — add nothing AI-related
- refs: YG-GAP-COH-001..003, Arch §10.1, ED-013
- gates: G1-G7

**Goal** Live/hybrid courses run as cohorts with enforced capacity and waitlist.
**Steps**
1. `cohorts`, `cohort_members`, `cohort_instructors`.
2. `ReserveCohortSeat` (row lock; throws when full), `JoinWaitlist`, `PromoteFromWaitlist`, `RemoveFromCohort`; hooks used later by checkout and manual grants.
3. Filament cohort management in course; member list; promote action.
**Acceptance**
- A1 Concurrent reservations never exceed capacity (test with parallel attempts / lock assertion).
- A2 Waitlist order respected on promotion (test).
- A3 Recorded-only course works without cohorts (test).
**Out of scope** Checkout integration (T-P8-12).

**Review requirement — YG-REV-PAY-003 (PRD §29.15)**
Capacity applies to every cohort admission source. Reserve/release seats atomically with bounded checkout holds; waitlist promotion consumes capacity. Do not auto-grant a late successful payment after the hold expired and seat was taken; record paid-but-unfulfilled review and notify finance/learner under O-031.

**Additional acceptance** Two buyers compete for final seat; duplicate waitlist promotion; expired/cancelled/failed hold release; manual grant/import at capacity; late callback; cohort cancellation. No oversell, silent lost payment or fictitious automatic refund. Evidence is required; this task remains todo until implemented and verified.

### T-P5-02 — Live session scheduling
- deps: T-P5-01, T-P3-04
- agents: implementer, ui-builder, test-writer
- size: M
- risk: normal
- ai: hook — live items marked non-eligible for AI
- refs: PRD §11, Roadmap M5.1, Checklist §7, ED-032
- gates: G1-G7

**Goal** Instructors/admins schedule live sessions for course, section or cohort with timezone-aware times.
**Steps**
1. `live_sessions` placed in the curriculum through `curriculum_items` (ED-002) (course, section?, cohort?, instructor, title/description translatable, starts_at/ends_at UTC, display timezone, provider manual_zoom/meet/bbb/custom, meeting_url encrypted, join window minutes, status lifecycle).
2. `MeetingProvider` contract with Manual driver.
3. Instructor scoped to assigned courses; lifecycle Actions (schedule, open, complete, cancel, reschedule) audited.
**Acceptance**
- A1 Instructor cannot schedule for unassigned course (test).
- A2 Times stored UTC; displayed in learner timezone (test).
**Out of scope** Reminders (T-P5-04).

**Review requirement — YG-REV-LRN-003 (PRD §29.15)**
Store UTC instants and IANA scheduling timezone; validate ambiguous/nonexistent local times. Rescheduling/cancellation invalidates old reminder jobs and join windows; no stale notification leaks protected meeting links. Attendance corrections are scoped and audited.

**Additional acceptance** Cairo daylight-saving transition, different learner timezone, reschedule after reminder queued, cancellation during open window, duplicate reminder job, wrong-cohort join and replay before publication. Evidence is required; this task remains todo until implemented and verified.
